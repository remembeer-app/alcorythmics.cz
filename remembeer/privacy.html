Remembeer Privacy Policy

Remembeer is a mobile application for keeping track of your drinks and drinking sessions, on your own or together with friends. This policy explains what personal data the app processes, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. The website privacy policy covers alcorythmics.cz itself.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Alcorythmics s.r.o., Company ID (IČO) 29927421, with its registered office at Bezručova 4201, 430 03 Chomutov, Czech Republic. You can reach us at info@alcorythmics.cz.

Who may use the app

Remembeer is intended for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. We do not knowingly process data of anyone younger; if you believe a minor has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

What data the app processes

Account. Your email address, the username you choose, an optional profile photo, your chosen accent colour, and the sign-in method you use. If you sign in with Google, we receive the email address and basic profile data that Google shares with us. Sign in with Apple will work the same way once it is available.

Drinks and sessions. Each drink you log: its type, volume, alcohol content and time. If you allow location access, the place where you logged it. Sessions you create, with their name, description, start and end time, and any photos you or other members add to a shared session. Custom drink types you define.

Social features. Your friends list and friend requests, the leaderboards you create or join, and your participation in Party sessions, including your drinks, points, challenge and beer-pong results within that party.

Derived statistics. Daily and monthly consumption totals and the badges you unlock, computed from your drinks.

Device and technical data. A push-notification token for your device, if you allow notifications. Our service provider, Google Firebase, also records technical data such as your IP address, device identifiers and request logs so that the service can operate and stay secure.

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking software. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for profiling or marketing.

Why we process it

We process account, drink, session, social and statistical data to provide the service you signed up for (performance of a contract). Location, photo and notification access are optional: the app asks for the corresponding permission on your device and you can withdraw it at any time in your device settings (consent). We process technical data to keep the service running, prevent abuse and diagnose faults (legitimate interest).

Who can see your data

Remembeer is a social app, so some of your data is visible to other users:

  • Other signed-in users can find you by username or email address and see your username, profile photo and the badges you choose to show.
  • Your friends can see the sessions you share with them.
  • Members of a shared session see all drinks, photos and details in that session.
  • Members of a leaderboard see each other's usernames and consumption totals for the ranked period.
  • Members of a Party session see each other's drinks, points, challenges and results.

Nobody outside the app sees your data, and we do not publish it.

Where your data is stored and who processes it

The app runs on Google Firebase, a service of Google LLC and Google Ireland Limited. We use Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Cloud Firestore (database), Cloud Storage (photos), Cloud Functions (server logic) and Cloud Messaging (push notifications). Your data is stored in Google's europe-west4 region in the Netherlands, within the European Union. Push notifications are delivered through Google's and Apple's notification services. If you sign in with Google or Apple, that provider processes your sign-in under its own policy. See the Firebase Privacy and Security page and the Google Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise it as described below. Firebase keeps technical logs for the periods set out in Google's documentation. If you email us, we keep the correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and then delete it.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app at any time under Settings → Delete account. This removes your sign-in, your profile details, your drinks and their locations, your solo sessions and their photos, your custom drinks, your friend links and requests, your settings and your notification token. Your drinks are also removed from shared sessions.

Because other people's data is intertwined with yours, a few things remain: shared sessions and leaderboards you owned pass to another member; photos you added to a shared session stay with that session; Party sessions keep your drinks, locations and scores, shown under the name “Deleted user” and no longer linked to any of your profile details. We plan to remove this residual Party data as well in a future version.

You can also ask us to delete your account by email.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, rectify or erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time. You may lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz). To exercise your rights, contact us at info@alcorythmics.cz.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's data processing changes and adjust the date at the top. For significant changes we will also tell you in the app.
